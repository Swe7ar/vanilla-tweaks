Your PC.

No Telemetry & No Hidden Background Services

We do not collect telemetry, track usage statistics, or display inflated/fake metrics.

100% Reversible Tweaks

Every tweak modifies documented registry keys or group policies. Revert any change at any time without reinstalling Windows.

Privacy & Transparency

1. Zero Data Harvesting: Vanilla Tweaks operates entirely locally on your Windows machine. We do not transmit system metrics, diagnostic telemetry, or personal files to external servers.

2. Discord OAuth Privacy: If you choose to log in with Discord, authentication is handled directly via Discord's secure OAuth2 service. Vanilla Tweaks only reads your public basic profile info (username and avatar) for local display.

3. Reversible System Tweaks: All optimizations are transparent modifications to standard Windows registry keys. System restore points are recommended and supported prior to batch updates.
